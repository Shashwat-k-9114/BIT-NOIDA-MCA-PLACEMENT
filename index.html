About BIT Mesra

Perched on the serene plateau of Ranchi, BIT Mesra is not merely an institution—it is a legacy. Founded in 1955 by the visionary industrialist Shri B.M. Birla, BIT Mesra stands as a citadel of excellence, a place where intellect, innovation, and integrity converge.

For decades, BIT Mesra has been synonymous with rigor, resilience, and relevance. It is a pioneer among technical institutions in India, known for its global outlook and unwavering pursuit of knowledge.

BIT Mesra Noida campus enjoys an advantageous position owing to its proximity to major industrial and IT hubs at Greater Noida, Gurugram, and Delhi. The campus was established in 1998 to meet the urgent need for an institution for diversified and advanced educational research, consultancy, and training in IT and Management technology in the Northern sector.

About BIT Noida

Birla Institute of Technology has over the years extended its expertise and scope of knowledge beyond its main campus at Mesra to overcome modern challenges in the field of IT and allied technology with proficiency.

The BIT Mesra Noida campus enjoys an advantageous position owing to its proximity to a major industrial and IT hub at Greater Noida, Gurugram, and Delhi. Strategically located near the border of Noida and Delhi, adjacent to many important industrial houses. The campus was established in 1998 after having felt the urgent need of an institution for diversified and advanced educational research, consultancy, and training of human resources in IT and Management technology in the Northern sector.

Our state-of the-art facilities are at the intersection of learning and hands-on-experience, which equip students to deep dive into reallife work situations. From advanced labs with high-end servers to a wide gamut of print and digital learning resources in the library, students have the wherewithal at their disposal to engage in quality learning. Besides this, the campus is equipped with recreational and club facilities that aid in the holistic development of students, preparing them for the world of tomorrow

Vision & Mission

Vision

To become a globally recognized academic institution in consonance with the social, economic and ecological environment, striving continuously for excellence in education, research and technological service to the national needs.

Mission

  • To educate students at Undergraduate, Post Graduate, Doctoral and Post Doctoral levels to perform challenging engineering and managerial jobs in industry
  • To provide excellent research and development facilities to take up Ph.D programmes and research projects
  • To develop effective teaching and learning skills and state-of-the-art research potential of the faculty
  • To build national capabilities in technology, education and research in emerging areas
  • To provide excellent technological services to satisfy the requirements of the industry and overall academic needs of society

Department of Computer Science & Applications

Overview

The Department of Computer Science and Applications endeavours to be an important regional, national, and international resource centre for the development of computing, IT systems and applications. The Department is recognized as one of the leading departments with infrastructure and facilities to match the very best in the country.

The department remains committed towards its mission, which is twofold. One is to provide students with the fundamental knowledge and problem solving skills in Computer Science required for a fulfilling career. The other goal is to create and disseminate knowledge to improve Computer Science research, education and practice.

Department Vision

The department strives to be recognized for outstanding education and research, leading to excellent professionals and innovators in the field of Computer Science and Engineering, who can positively contribute to the society.

Department Mission

  • To impart quality education and equip the students with strong foundation that could make them capable of handling challenges of the new century.
  • To maintain state of the art research facilities and facilitate interaction with world's leading universities, industries and research organization for constant improvement in the quality of education and research.
